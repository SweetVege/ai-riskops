# AI RiskOps Project Delivery Summary

## One-Line Positioning

AI RiskOps is an enterprise AI application risk operations platform that helps teams monitor, analyze, and explain risks generated by LLM, RAG, Copilot, and Agent applications.

## Product Objective

The product is designed as an AI risk observability and operations layer, not a full audit or remediation workflow system. Its core purpose is to record model-call activity, detect potential risk events, explain why risks occurred, and help enterprise teams understand where AI application risk is increasing.

## Target Users

| User Profile | Primary Need | Product Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Global User | Understand enterprise-wide AI risk posture | View global dashboards, analytics, events, logs, and application profiles |
| App Owner | Monitor risks for owned AI applications | View only assigned application data across overview, analytics, events, logs, and application profiles |
| Platform Admin | Configure the AI RiskOps platform | Manage policies, application setup, credentials, ingestion audit, and user access |

## Delivered Product Capabilities

### Executive Risk Overview

- AI application risk posture dashboard with model-call volume, risk event count, blocked event count, and average event risk score.
- Daily, monthly, and quarterly severity trends with High+Severe Rate overlay.
- Risk category distribution, top risky applications, and severe event snapshots with entry points into detailed investigation.

### Risk Analytics

- Risk metric framework covering Risk Event Rate, High+Severe Rate, Average Risk Score, Block Rate, Rule Hit Rate, and Log Coverage Score.
- Driver analysis by application, risk category, matched rule, environment, user role, and data type.
- Drill-down workflows and LLM-style insight summaries to help analysts explain metric movement and identify likely contributors behind severe-risk increases.

### Risk Event Workbench

- Risk event list and detail view built around evidence-based investigation.
- Event attributes include severity, system action, human review status, SLA, owner, affected asset, and matched rules.
- Matched Rules & Evidence section connects detection logic with concrete model-call evidence.

### Call Logs

- Model-call log records for monitored AI applications.
- Prompt, output, metadata, action, linked event references, and masked sensitive content in standard responses.
- API-backed filtering foundation for future operational log analysis.

### Applications

- Application inventory and application-level risk profiles.
- Application-scoped data visibility for App Owner users.
- Connected, validating, and pending-validation states for monitored AI applications.

### Admin

- Policy Center for detection templates and rule enablement.
- Application Setup for onboarding and ingestion configuration.
- Credential creation, rotation, and revocation for application ingestion.
- Ingestion audit logs and user access configuration.

## Interview Pitch

AI RiskOps started from a practical enterprise problem: companies are adopting LLM, RAG, and Agent applications faster than their governance teams can understand the risk posture. I designed the product as an observability and risk operations layer that records AI calls, detects risk events, explains matched evidence, and helps different users understand risk from the right scope.

The first version focuses on making risk visible and explainable. Governance leaders can see trends and hotspots, analysts can drill into drivers and events, App Owners can monitor only their own applications, and Platform Admins can configure policies, ingestion, credentials, and access. I also moved the prototype from frontend-only mock data to an API-backed online demo using Next.js, Prisma, Neon Postgres, GitHub Actions, and Vercel.
